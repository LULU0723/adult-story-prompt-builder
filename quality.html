Automated Quality Gate

Runs the existing regression suite and Coverage Lint, applies foundation rules, then compares stable 2/3/2 pool metrics against the frozen coverage baseline.

Current rules

Hard fail: foundation gate failures plus a tracked 2/3/2 config disappearing, eligibleItems decreasing, or avgEligiblePool / per-stage pool falling more than 15% below baseline.

Warning: foundation warnings plus fixture-count/dataVersion changes or loss of previously observed mobility/preservation behavior.

1/1/1 pool metrics are intentionally excluded from the numeric baseline. preservationRejections and mobilityRunRatio are tracked only as observed/not-observed signals.
